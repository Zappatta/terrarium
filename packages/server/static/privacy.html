Privacy

Terrarium, a hobby project run by Yoske (zappatta@gmail.com). Last updated 31 October 2026.

What we collect

Watching worlds, replays and leaderboards needs no account and collects nothing about you.

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture link. Google tells us these; we never see your Google password.

We also store what you create: the code of creatures you upload, the matches they play, and the API keys you make. API keys and sign-in sessions are stored only as one-way hashes, so even we cannot read them back.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in, with one session cookie.
  • To show your name next to your creatures and on leaderboards. Your email address is not shown to other people.
  • Your creatures' source code and debug logs are visible only to you and the site admin.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, and no selling or sharing of your data. The only other company involved is Google, for the sign-in itself.

Where it lives

On a single privately run server. The code is public at github.com/Zappatta/terrarium.

Deleting your data

Email zappatta@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and your account, creatures and keys will be deleted.
